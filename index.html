Nobody Remembers Rain

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Nobody Remembers Rain is a single-player game made by Dejan Črnila. This policy covers the Android app, the desktop app and the version that plays in a web browser.

In short

The game does not collect, store on any server, or share any information about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What the game keeps, and where

The game saves your progress (your settlement, your current run, the Notebook and your options) only on your own device: in the app's own storage on Android and on the desktop, or in your browser's storage when you play in a browser. It is never sent to me or to anyone else.

You can delete it at any time: use Options → Reset the game, uninstall the app, or clear the browser's data for the game's page.

The internet

The game plays fully offline. It does not send anything over the internet: no personal data, no device identifiers, no usage statistics and no crash reports.

Android backups

On Android, your phone's own backup (Google's Android backup, if you have it turned on) may include the game's save along with your other apps' data, so it comes back when you set up a new phone. That backup is run by Google under your Google account and its settings, not by the game, and I cannot see it.

Where you get the game

Google Play, itch.io and other stores you download the game from have their own privacy policies for what they collect when you use them (for example your purchase or download history). The game itself adds nothing to that.

Children

The game collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If the game ever starts to collect or share anything, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
